About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at Brown University, advised by Kavita Ramanan.

My research interests are in probability, with a focus on sparse random graphs, large deviations, interacting particle systems, and their connections to statistical physics and inference.

You can reach me at i-hsun_chen@brown.edu.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral positions starting in fall 2027.
